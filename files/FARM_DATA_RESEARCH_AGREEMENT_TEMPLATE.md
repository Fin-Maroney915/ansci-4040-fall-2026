## 2. Data Covered

The data covered by this Agreement (**“Farm Data”**) is listed in **Schedule A** and may include, as selected by the Parties:

- production, yield, crop, livestock, soil, input, machinery, sensor, weather, financial, labor, animal-health, or management data;
- field boundaries, coordinates, maps, and other geospatial information;
- photographs, records, survey responses, interview materials, or samples and related measurements; and
- metadata necessary to understand and responsibly reuse the Farm Data.

Farm Data does not include information outside Schedule A unless the Parties add it through a written amendment.

## 3. Farmer Authority and Voluntary Participation

The Farmer represents that the Farmer has authority to provide the Farm Data identified in Schedule A or has disclosed any third-party rights or restrictions. Participation is voluntary. Refusal to participate or a later withdrawal will not result in a penalty or loss of benefits to which the Farmer is otherwise entitled, except that completed analyses or published results generally cannot be withdrawn.

The Farmer is not required to provide information unrelated to the research purpose. The Researcher will use reasonable efforts to minimize the amount and sensitivity of Farm Data collected.

## 4. Ownership and Limited Permission to Use

As between the Parties, the Farmer retains all rights and interests the Farmer holds in the original Farm Data. No ownership of the Farmer’s land, animals, equipment, business records, trade secrets, or intellectual property is transferred.

The Farmer grants the Researcher a **[non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free]** permission to collect, copy, clean, combine, analyze, preserve, and use the Farm Data only for the purpose and term described in this Agreement. The Researcher may share Farm Data only as allowed by Sections 7 and 8.

Subject to applicable course and institutional policies, the Student Researcher owns the Student Researcher’s original writing, code, documentation, and analyses, while the Farmer retains the Farmer’s rights in the underlying Farm Data. ## 6. Data Quality and Context

The Farmer will provide Farm Data in the form reasonably available and is not responsible for errors that were not knowingly introduced. The Researcher will document material cleaning, transformations, assumptions, missing values, limitations, and provenance. The Researcher will not knowingly present conclusions in a misleading manner or attribute errors to the Farmer without appropriate verification and context.

## 7. Confidentiality, Privacy, and Publication

### 7.1 Confidential treatment

The Researcher will treat identifiable Farm Data, precise locations, financial information, trade secrets, and other information marked or reasonably understood as confidential (**“Confidential Farm Data”**) as confidential. Access will be limited to authorized personnel who need it for the project and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.

Confidentiality does not apply to information that the Researcher can document: (a) was lawfully public without breach of this Agreement; (b) was already lawfully known without a duty of confidentiality; (c) was independently developed without use of Confidential Farm Data; or (d) was lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

### 7.2 De-identification and aggregation

Before public release, the Researcher will use reasonable measures to remove direct identifiers and reduce re-identification risk, including risks created by exact field locations, rare practices, small groups, or linking with other datasets. Public reporting will use **[aggregation threshold, e.g., at least five farms]** unless the Farmer gives written permission for a different approach.

The Researcher will not attempt to re-identify de-identified Farm Data or publicly identify the Farmer or farm without the Farmer’s prior written consent.

### 7.3 Publication and review

The Student Researcher may submit aggregated or sufficiently de-identified results to the course instructor, Faculty Supervisor, and others listed in Schedule A. At least **[30] days** before any public publication or presentation, the Student Researcher will provide the Farmer any portion that identifies the Farmer or describes the farm in a reasonably recognizable way. The Farmer may request correction of factual errors, removal of identifiers, or protection of confidential information, but may not suppress good-faith research findings solely because they are unfavorable.

Credit or acknowledgment will be handled as follows: **[anonymous / named acknowledgment with consent / authorship under stated criteria].**

### 7.4 Required disclosure

If disclosure is required by law, subpoena, court order, public-records law, or funder/institutional rule, the Researcher will, to the extent legally permitted, promptly notify the Farmer and disclose only what is required. ## 8. FAIR Data Stewardship

The Parties intend to manage research data according to the FAIR principles: **Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable**. FAIR does **not** automatically mean open or public. Confidential or sensitive Farm Data may remain protected through controlled access while its metadata and stewardship practices support appropriate discovery and reuse.

### 8.1 Findable

The Researcher will:

- assign a stable project identifier and, when appropriate, a persistent identifier to approved datasets or outputs;
- create sufficiently detailed, machine-readable metadata describing provenance, time period, geography at an appropriate level, variables, methods, quality limits, and access conditions; and
- register or index approved metadata in **[repository/catalog]**, without exposing confidential details.

### 8.2 Accessible

The Researcher will:

- document how authorized users may request or retrieve data and metadata using standard, secure methods;
- use access levels selected in Schedule A (closed, controlled, embargoed, or open);
- require authentication, a data-use agreement, ethics approval, or Farmer consent where appropriate; and
- preserve non-sensitive metadata when feasible even if the underlying Farm Data is later deleted or access ends.

### 8.3 Interoperable

The Researcher will, where practical:

- use non-proprietary or widely used formats such as **[CSV, JSON, GeoJSON, TIFF, or other]**;
- use shared units, data dictionaries, controlled vocabularies, and relevant agricultural standards identified in Schedule B;
- record relationships among datasets, samples, methods, software, and publications; and
- provide documented conversion methods when a proprietary source format must be retained.

### 8.4 Reusable

The Researcher will:

- preserve provenance, collection methods, variable definitions, quality-control steps, known limitations, and version history;
- attach clear, machine-readable access and reuse terms;
- allow reuse only for **[the original project / approved related research / specified fields]** and prohibit unauthorized commercial, regulatory, insurance, lending, marketing, or enforcement uses;
- require downstream users to protect confidentiality, avoid re-identification, cite the dataset or project where appropriate, and comply with the same or stricter restrictions; and
- use the following license for any data expressly approved for release: **[license or custom terms; do not select an open license for data the Farmer has not approved for public reuse].**

The FAIR implementation choices, repositories, standards, and access levels are recorded in **Schedule B** and may not override the Farmer protections in this Agreement.

## 9. Prohibited Uses

Unless the Farmer separately agrees in writing, the Researcher and all authorized users must not use Farm Data:

- to make decisions about the Farmer’s credit, insurance, eligibility, pricing, employment, land value, regulatory status, or legal liability;
- for advertising, sale, unrelated commercial product development, any commercial activity, or training a general-purpose artificial-intelligence model;
- to contact the Farmer for unrelated solicitation;
- to re-identify the Farmer, farm, workers, customers, or business partners;
- to establish ownership or control over the Farmer’s original Farm Data; or
- in a manner that violates applicable law, institutional approval, or this Agreement.

Additional prohibited uses: **[Insert any project-specific restrictions].**

## 10. Security and Incident Response

The Researcher will maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards proportionate to the risk, including:

- encrypted transfer and storage where appropriate;
- least-privilege access, unique user accounts, and multi-factor authentication;
- access logs, backups, patching, and secure configuration;
- separation or coding of direct identifiers where practical;
- confidentiality and security training for authorized personnel; and
- written procedures for incident response and secure deletion.

The Researcher will notify the Farmer without unreasonable delay and, where feasible, within **[number] hours/days** after confirming unauthorized access, use, loss, or disclosure that materially affects Farm Data. Notice will describe the known facts, likely risks, response steps, and a contact person, subject to lawful investigative restrictions.

## 11. Service Providers and Other Researchers

Approved recipients and service providers are listed in Schedule A. They may include the Faculty Supervisor, course instructor, or authorized project teammates if specifically named or described. The Student Researcher may not provide identifiable or Confidential Farm Data to another party unless that party needs access for the stated purpose and is bound by applicable institutional confidentiality duties or written restrictions at least as protective as this Agreement. The Student Researcher remains responsible for managing approved recipients consistent with this Agreement.

No sale or licensing of identifiable Farm Data is permitted without the Farmer’s separate, specific, written consent.

## 12. Return of Results and Corrections

The Researcher will provide the Farmer with **[a plain-language summary / farm-level results / dataset copy / other]** by **[date or project milestone]**, subject to scientific validity and third-party restrictions. The Farmer may submit corrections or contextual information. The Researcher will document material corrections and, where feasible, update affected datasets or outputs without altering the integrity of the research record.

## 13. Term, Withdrawal, Retention, and Destruction

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends on **[date/event]**, unless ended earlier under this section.

The Farmer may withdraw permission for future collection and use by written notice to **[contact]**. Within **[30/60/90] days**, the Researcher will stop new use and securely delete or return identifiable Farm Data, except for:

- data already included in completed analyses, publications, or legally required research records;
- data that has been irreversibly de-identified so it can no longer reasonably be linked to the Farmer; and
- limited archival backups that cannot reasonably be isolated, provided they remain protected, are not restored for active use, and are deleted under the normal backup cycle.

At the end of the retention period, **[date or period]**, the Researcher will **[securely delete / return / archive under controlled access]** the Farm Data and document the action. Any retained metadata must avoid exposing confidential information.

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for a material breach if the breach is not cured within **[30] days** after written notice, or immediately when continued use would create a serious legal, privacy, security, animal-welfare, or safety risk.

Before collecting Farm Data, the Student Researcher will consult the Faculty Supervisor or other appropriate institutional contact to determine whether institutional review, informed-consent, animal-care, biosafety, data-protection, or other approval is required. The Student Researcher will not represent that the project is institutionally approved unless approval has actually been granted. The Parties will comply with applicable laws and binding obligations. If Farm Data includes personal information about employees, family members, customers, veterinarians, or other individuals, the Parties will identify the lawful collection and use process in Schedule A.
